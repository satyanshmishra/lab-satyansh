The material scanner

Pick an object. Shine a torch on it to check for lustre, then try to scratch it with an iron nail and press it with your fingers.

Can light pass through?

Hold each object in front of a torch and look at the picture behind it. Then decide: transparent, translucent or opaque?

Transparent: we can see clearly through it. Translucent: we can see, but not clearly. Opaque: we cannot see through it at all.

Mass and volume

Three identical cups are half filled with water (A), sand (B) and pebbles (C). Put any two on the balance. Then pour water into a measuring cylinder.

0 mL volume of water

Water takes up space. The space it occupies is its volume.

What is matter?

Anything that has mass and occupies space (has volume) is called matter. Water, sand, pebbles, and even air are matter.

Check what you learnt

Lost lustreSome metals lose their shine due to air and moisture. We often see the lustre only on a freshly cut surface.
Right material, right jobA tumbler can be made of glass, steel or clay, but not of paper or cloth. Materials are chosen for their properties.
Is air matter?Yes! A blown-up balloon is heavier than an empty one, and air fills the space inside it.
