Inside ice, water and vapour

Change the temperature and watch the tiny particles of water. Then pour it into a container of a different shape.

Solid

Melting: solid → liquid. Freezing: liquid → solid.
Evaporation: liquid → gas. Condensation: gas → liquid.

Check what you learnt

Clouds need dustWater vapour condenses on tiny dust particles in the air. That is how cloud droplets form.
HumidityThe amount of water vapour in the air is called humidity. Clothes dry slowly on humid days.
Why sweat cools youAs sweat evaporates, it takes heat from your skin. That is the cooling effect of evaporation.
