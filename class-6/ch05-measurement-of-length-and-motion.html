Reading a scale the correct way

The pencil may not start at the zero mark. Move your eye and see how the reading seems to change. Then measure the pencil and type your answer.

Length =cm

Three rules

1. Place the scale along the object, touching it.
2. Keep your eye exactly above the mark you read.
3. If the zero mark is broken, start at another full mark and subtract.

Check what you learnt

Ancient Indian unitsAngula (finger width), dhanusa and yojana were used in India in ancient times to measure length.
Number and unitA measurement has two parts: a number and a unit. In “13 handspans”, 13 is the number and handspan is the unit.
Rest or motion?An object is in motion if its position changes with time compared with a reference point.
