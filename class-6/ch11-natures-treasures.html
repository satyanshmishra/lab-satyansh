How fossil fuels formed

Drag the slider through millions of years and see how coal and petroleum formed deep under the ground.

Coal, petroleum and natural gas are fossil fuels. Petrol and diesel, the most common fuels for vehicles, are made from petroleum.

Renewable or non-renewable?

Renewable resources are replenished within a reasonable time. Non-renewable ones take millions of years to form, or cannot be replaced, and can run out.
