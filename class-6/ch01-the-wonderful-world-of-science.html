The Wonderful World of Science

Science is a way of thinking, observing and doing things. In this lab you will solve mysteries the way scientists do: observe, ask, guess, test and analyse.

  1. Solve the pen mystery
  2. Think like a repair expert

  5. Check what you learnt

Why did my pen stop writing?

Your pen suddenly stops writing. Find the real reason by guessing and testing, one guess at a time. The cause is hidden and changes every round.
