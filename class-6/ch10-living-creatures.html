Living Creatures: Exploring their Characteristics

What makes something alive? Test the signs of life, grow bean seeds under different conditions, watch shoots chase light, and follow the life cycles of a frog and a mosquito.

The signs-of-life checker

Pick something. Tick every sign of life it shows, then decide. Living beings show all of these signs.

Living beings…

need food and water, grow, breathe (respire), get rid of wastes (excrete), respond to stimuli, reproduce, and have a life span. Many can move from place to place.

Plants respire too: they take in air through tiny pores called stomata.

Life cycles

Put the stages in order. Then watch the whole life cycle play.

The larva or pupa puzzle

Puddle water has both larvae and pupae of mosquitoes. To find which comes first after the egg, keep larvae alone in a jar and observe each day. The larvae change into pupae, so larva comes before pupa.

Check what you learnt

Seeds are aliveA seed seems non-living, but given water, air and the right temperature it grows into a plant.
Life spanEvery living being lives only for a certain period of time, called its life span.
Stop the breedingMosquitoes lay eggs in still water. Emptying coolers, pots and tyres stops their life cycle.
