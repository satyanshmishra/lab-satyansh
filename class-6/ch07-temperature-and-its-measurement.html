Reading a laboratory thermometer

Heat or cool the water and watch the liquid column. Then read the temperature and type it in. Range: −10 °C to 110 °C. Each small division is 1 °C.

Reading:°C

Rules

Keep the thermometer upright. The bulb must be surrounded by water and must not touch the bottom or sides. Read it while it is still in the water, with your eye level with the top of the column.

Which thermometer should you use?

A clinical thermometer reads from 35 °C to 42 °C. A laboratory thermometer reads from −10 °C to 110 °C. Pick a task, then pick a thermometer.

Check what you learnt

Not exactly 37 °CNormal body temperature differs slightly from person to person. The average is taken as 37.0 °C.
Boiling in ShillongPhiban and her classmates in Shillong measured boiling water at about 98 °C, not 100 °C. Water boils at a lower temperature at higher altitudes.
Say no to mercuryMercury is extremely toxic, so mercury thermometers are being replaced by digital ones.
