What I found

Soak and split seeds

Chickpea and maize seeds were soaked in water for two days. Tap a seed to remove its coat and open it. Count the cotyledons (seed leaves).

Seeds with two cotyledons are called dicots. Seeds with one cotyledon are called monocots.

Link it up

Dicot plants like chickpea usually have reticulate venation and a taproot. Monocot plants like maize usually have parallel venation and fibrous roots.

Where do they live?

The place where an organism lives is its habitat. Special features that help it survive there are called adaptations. Tap each glowing plant or animal.

Tap a glowing living thing

Each one shows a feature that helps it live in this habitat.

Habitats are of two kinds

Terrestrial (on land): deserts, mountains, forests, grasslands.
Aquatic (in water): ponds, lakes, rivers, oceans.

Two humps in the coldCamels of the hot desert have one hump; camels of the cold desert have two. Humps store fat for times when food is scarce.
