Key ideas

Luminous objects emit light; the Moon only reflects sunlight.
Light travels in a straight line.
Transparent: light passes fully; translucent: partly; opaque: not at all.
A shadow needs a light source, an opaque object and a screen.
A plane mirror’s image is erect, same size, laterally inverted and can’t be caught on a screen.
A pinhole camera forms an inverted image.
