Writing units correctly

Second s, minute min, hour h — lowercase, singular, no full stop, with a space: 5 s, not “5sec” or “5 hrs”. 1 km/h = 5/18 m/s.

Key ideas

All clocks use a process that repeats (or flows) steadily.
Time period = time for one complete oscillation.
A pendulum’s time period depends on its length, not the mass of the bob.
SI unit of time: second (s). 60 s = 1 min, 60 min = 1 h.
Speed = total distance ÷ total time; SI unit m/s.
Uniform linear motion: equal distances in equal intervals of time.
