Key ideas

Conduction: heat passes particle to particle; particles stay in place (mainly in solids).
Convection: heat is carried by moving particles of liquids and gases.
Radiation needs no medium — the Sun’s heat reaches us this way.
Land heats and cools faster than water → sea breeze by day, land breeze at night.
Air is a poor conductor — woollens, two thin blankets and hollow bricks keep us comfortable.
Water seeps fastest through gravel and slowest through clay; it is stored in aquifers.
