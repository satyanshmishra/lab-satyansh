Neutralisation in a Test Tube

A drop of lemon juice in water with blue litmus solution turns red. Now add lime water drop by drop and swirl. Can you find the moment the acid is neutralised?

ObservationThe solution is red — it is acidic.

The reaction

Acid + Base → Salt + Water + Heat

When an acid and a base are mixed in sufficient quantity, the solution becomes neither acidic nor basic. This is a neutralisation reaction; heat is given out.

Key ideas

Acids turn blue litmus red; bases turn red litmus blue.
Red rose extract: red in acids, green in bases.
Turmeric turns red only in bases — it can't tell acid from neutral.
Litmus comes from lichens; many flowers and vegetables are natural indicators.
Acid + Base → Salt + Water + Heat (neutralisation).
Ant bites, acidic soil and acidic factory waste are treated by neutralisation.
