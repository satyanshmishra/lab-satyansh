The Spinning Earth: Day, Night & the Moving Sun

Merry-go-round idea

On a merry-go-round turning anti-clockwise, trees seem to move clockwise past you. Aryabhata said the same: like a man in a boat sees the shore moving backwards, we see the stars moving west.

Myth check

Seasons are not caused by the Earth being nearer to the Sun — in fact Earth is closest to the Sun in January! They are caused by the tilt and the round shape of the Earth.

Check Yourself

Key ideas

Earth rotates West → East in about 24 h, causing day, night and the apparent motion of Sun, Moon and stars.
The Pole Star stays nearly still; other stars circle it.
Earth revolves around the Sun in about 365¼ days; different stars are seen in different months.
Seasons come from the tilted axis and Earth’s round shape.
Solar eclipse: Moon blocks the Sun. Lunar eclipse: Earth’s shadow falls on the Moon.
The Moon looks as big as the Sun because it is much closer.
