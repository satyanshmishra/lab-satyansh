Breathing vs respiration

Breathing (physical): moving air in and out of the lungs. Respiration (chemical): inside the body, Glucose + Oxygen → Carbon dioxide + Water + Energy.

Station 6 · Sections 9.1.2 & 9.2.2

Animal Variety

Animals have different body parts to digest food and to breathe, suited to their food and habitat. Sort each animal by how it breathes.

Ruminants (cow, buffalo)

They partially chew grass and swallow it into the stomach (rumen) where partial digestion happens. The food is brought back to the mouth and chewed again — rumination. A cow spends about 8 hours a day chewing!

Birds

Birds have no teeth. A muscular chamber called the gizzard grinds food — often with the help of small stones (grit) the bird swallows.

Key ideas

Digestion breaks complex food into simpler forms along the alimentary canal.
Saliva starts breaking starch into sugar in the mouth.
Stomach: digestive juice, acid and mucus. Liver gives bile; pancreas gives pancreatic juice.
Nutrients are absorbed in the small intestine (~6 m long); the large intestine absorbs water.
Diaphragm down + ribs out = inhale; diaphragm up + ribs in = exhale.
Gas exchange happens in the alveoli. Fish use gills, earthworms use skin.
