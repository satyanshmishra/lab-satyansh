Sonority all around

The school bell, temple bells, ghungroos and the strings of a sitar or veena ring because metals are sonorous. A visually impaired person can even tell metal from wood by the sound their stick makes!

Fighting rust

Rusting is prevented by painting, oiling, greasing or coating iron with zinc (galvanisation). The 1600-year-old Iron Pillar of Delhi has barely rusted — a wonder of ancient Indian metallurgy!

Station 6

Metal, Non-metal — or Not an Element?

Metals and non-metals are both elements — substances that cannot be broken into simpler substances (118 are known). Plastic, wood, glass and rubber are not elements at all!

Station 7

Check Yourself

Key ideas

Metals are generally lustrous, hard, malleable, ductile and sonorous.
Metals are good conductors of heat and electricity; non-metals generally are not.
Metal oxides are basic; non-metal oxides are acidic.
Iron rusts only when both air and water are present (moist air).
Corrosion: green coat on copper, black coat on silver, rust on iron.
Oxygen, carbon, nitrogen, chlorine and iodine are useful non-metals.
