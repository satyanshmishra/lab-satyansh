Key ideas

Earth is at a distance from the Sun where water can stay liquid — the habitable or Goldilocks zone.
Earth’s nearly circular orbit keeps it from getting too hot or too cold during the year.
Earth’s gravity holds the atmosphere; the ozone layer blocks harmful ultraviolet rays.
Earth’s magnetic field shields us from high-energy particles from the Sun.
Atmosphere, hydrosphere, geosphere and biosphere interact to sustain life.
Reproduction (asexual or sexual) ensures the continuity of life.
Climate change, biodiversity loss and pollution together form the triple planetary crisis.
