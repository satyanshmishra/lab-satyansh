Key ideas

A mixture has two or more substances that keep their own properties and can be separated physically.
A pure substance is made of only one kind of particle.
Elements cannot be broken down into simpler substances; they are the building blocks of matter.
A compound is made of two or more elements chemically combined in a fixed ratio, with new properties.
Water is a compound of hydrogen and oxygen; sugar is a compound of carbon, hydrogen and oxygen.
Minerals are natural solids with a fixed chemical composition.
