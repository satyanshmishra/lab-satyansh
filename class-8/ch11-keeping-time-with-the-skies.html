Key ideas

The Moon has no light of its own; we see the part of its sunlit half that faces us.
A full cycle of phases takes about 29.5 days.
The Moon rises about 50 minutes later each day.
A lunar year of 12 months is about 354 days; a solar year is about 365¼ days.
Luni-solar calendars add an extra month (adhik maas) now and then to stay in step with the seasons.
The Indian National Calendar is a solar calendar; its year begins on 22 March (21 March in a leap year).
Artificial satellites help with weather, communication, navigation and studying space.
