Key ideas

A force is a push or a pull on an object due to its interaction with another object.
The SI unit of force is the newton (N).
A force can change the speed, the direction of motion, or the shape of an object.
Friction acts opposite to the direction of motion and is larger on rough surfaces.
Magnetic, electrostatic and gravitational forces act without contact.
Weight is the force of gravity on an object; mass stays the same everywhere, weight changes.
A liquid pushes up on an object placed in it — upthrust (buoyant force).
