Key ideas

A habitat provides the right conditions for an organism to live and grow.
An ecosystem is formed by the interaction of biotic (living) and abiotic (non-living) components.
Producers make their own food; consumers eat plants or animals; decomposers break down dead matter.
Food chains show who eats whom; linked food chains form a food web.
Removing one organism can upset the whole ecosystem.
Mutualism: both benefit · Commensalism: one benefits, other unaffected · Parasitism: one benefits, other harmed.
Healthy ecosystems give us clean air, water, food, medicines and a stable climate.
