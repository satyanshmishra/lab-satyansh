How it worksGrains are spread fairly evenly, so a small sample tells us about the whole tray. More samples → better estimate.

Sand or stars?

Scientists estimate that our galaxy, the Milky Way, has a few hundred billion stars (about 1011). Using sampling, the sand grains on all beaches and deserts are estimated at around 1018 — millions of times more!

But the whole universe has far more stars than Earth has sand grains.

Check Yourself

Key ideas

Investigation starts with a focused question that can be tested.
In a fair test, change only ONE thing and keep everything else the same.
The thing we change is a variable; we measure what happens to another variable.
Repeat a test to make sure the result is reliable.
Scientists estimate huge numbers by counting a small sample and scaling up.
Each answer in science opens up new questions.
