Diffusion around you

The smell of an agarbatti or of hot food spreads across a room because gas particles move fast and mix with air particles. Hot food smells reach you faster than cold food!

Key ideas

Matter is made of extremely tiny particles.
Particles are held together by interparticle forces of attraction.
Attraction is strongest in solids, weaker in liquids and weakest in gases.
Solids have a fixed shape and volume; liquids have a fixed volume but no fixed shape; gases have neither.
Gases are easily compressed because their particles have lots of space between them.
Particles are always moving; they move faster when heated, so diffusion is faster in hot water.
