Key ideas

The cell is the basic unit of life.
A cell has a cell membrane, cytoplasm and a nucleus.
Plant, fungal and bacterial cells also have a cell wall.
Bacteria do not have a well-defined nucleus.
The shape of a cell is related to its job.
Bacteria and protozoa are unicellular; fungi can be unicellular or multicellular.
Viruses reproduce only inside a host’s cells.
Lactobacillus makes curd; yeast makes dough rise; Rhizobium in root nodules fixes nitrogen.
