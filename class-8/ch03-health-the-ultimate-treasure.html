Key ideas

Health means physical, mental and social well-being — not just the absence of disease.
Symptoms are what we feel; signs can be seen or measured.
Communicable (infectious) diseases are caused by pathogens and can spread from person to person.
Non-communicable diseases like diabetes are linked to lifestyle and environment, not germs.
Handwashing, clean water, covering coughs and keeping surroundings clean break the chain of spread.
Vaccines train the immune system using dead, weakened or harmless parts of a germ.
