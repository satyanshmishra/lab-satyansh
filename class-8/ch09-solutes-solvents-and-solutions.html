Key ideas

A solution is a uniform mixture; the substance dissolved is the solute and the one that dissolves it is the solvent.
A saturated solution cannot dissolve any more solute at that temperature.
Solubility is the maximum amount of solute that dissolves in 100 mL of solvent at a given temperature.
Usually, solubility of solids increases with temperature, while that of gases decreases.
Density = mass ÷ volume; its unit can be g/mL (or g/cm³).
Objects less dense than water float; those more dense sink.
