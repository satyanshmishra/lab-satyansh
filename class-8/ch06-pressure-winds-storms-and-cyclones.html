Key ideas

Pressure is force per unit area; its SI unit is N/m², called the pascal (Pa).
The same force on a smaller area gives more pressure.
Liquids and gases exert pressure on the walls of their container; liquid pressure increases with depth.
Air around us exerts atmospheric pressure.
Wind blows from high pressure to low pressure; warm air rises, creating low pressure.
Lightning is caused by electric charges built up in clouds; a lightning conductor protects buildings.
Cyclones form over warm seas around a centre of very low pressure; IMD monitors them.
